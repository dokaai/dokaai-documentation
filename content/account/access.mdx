---
title: "Get access to Dokaai"
description: "Sign in, verify your email, accept an invitation, and recover account access."
---

Start by opening the Dokaai application used by your team. Your account sign-in and your membership in an organization/project are separate: signing in does not automatically give you access to every team's data.

## Create an account or sign in

Use the sign-up form if you need an account. Enter the identity/contact details requested and choose a password that meets the requirements shown by the form. Complete email verification using the code sent to the address you registered. If you already have an account, use the sign-in screen.

If the application reports that pilot access is not enabled for your email, follow the access/waitlist instructions shown or ask your team administrator. Do not assume creating an account also grants pilot approval, service subscription or project membership.

## Accept an invitation

1. Open the invitation link sent by your team.
2. Check the invited email and organization/project context presented by the application.
3. Sign in with the intended account, or complete account creation and verification if requested.
4. Complete the invitation acceptance action and check that the expected organization/project is available.

If the link is invalid, the account is wrong or the invitation cannot be accepted, ask the inviter to review its state and resend it where available. Keep invitation links private. Administrators can use [Members and roles](/account/members-and-roles) to manage invitations and access.

## Recover access

Use **Forgot password** from the sign-in flow, enter the intended email address, and follow the reset instructions received by email. Choose a new password that meets the reset form's requirements. An accepted reset request is separate from completing the reset and signing in successfully.

Once signed in, continue with [organization and project setup](/account/setup). If you can sign in but a resource is missing, check membership and the selected account context before changing API credentials.
