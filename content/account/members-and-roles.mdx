---
title: "Invite Members"
description: "Invite members and review organization and project access separately."
---

Use the organization and project member areas to give teammates access to the work they manage. You need access to the selected account context and permission to manage its members.

## Join a team as an invited member

Receiving an invitation and accepting it are separate steps. Use the invited email account, complete sign-in/verification if requested, and accept the intended organization/project invitation. [Get access to Dokaai](/account/access) explains that flow and password recovery.

## Invite a member

1. Select the organization and open **Org Members**, or open the intended project's **Project Members** area.
2. Use the invitation action and enter the teammate's email address.
3. Choose from the roles offered for that context and submit the invitation.
4. Review the invitation or membership state shown in the member list.

An invitation request is not confirmation that the person has accepted it. Check the displayed state before assuming access is ready.

## Review a project role

Open the project's member list, identify the member, and use the available role-management controls to update their project assignment. Recheck the resulting role in that project.

Organization-level and project-level roles are separate configuration contexts. Use the roles actually offered by the dashboard; this guide does not prescribe a universal permission matrix.

If a teammate cannot see the expected data, first check the selected organization/project and their membership there. Continue with [account setup](/account/setup) or [handler configuration](/build/handlers).
