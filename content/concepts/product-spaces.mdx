A product space represents an app or brand within your organization. Associate a project with a product space to identify the product context for its notification configuration. The dashboard lets multiple projects use the same product space.

## Example

Acme runs a shopping app and a seller portal. It can create product spaces named **Acme Shop** and **Acme Seller**. Related projects can be associated with Acme Shop when they belong to that app. A separate brand or app can use a different product space.

The product space has a name, description, ID and code. The name helps operators recognize it; APIs and customer-authentication configuration use the identifiers required by their contracts.

## Why it matters

Product spaces appear in project setup, customer signing-key management and the hosted unsubscribe experience. When you configure a customer-facing integration, keep the selected product space consistent with its signing configuration and customer identity.

## Changing an association

Switching an existing project's product space is a significant configuration change. The dashboard warns that it may invalidate handler customer-preference settings and requires confirmation. Review affected handlers and their preference associations after a switch. Do not use a product-space switch as a routine way to rename a project.
