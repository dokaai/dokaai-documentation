A service is a Dokaai product your organization can subscribe to and make available in a project. These notification guides describe the notification service. The service list is supplied by your account; a card marked Coming soon does not mean the product is ready to use.

## Two setup steps

1. **Organization subscription:** the organization subscribes to a service and manages its billing state.
2. **Project enablement:** the subscribed service is enabled for a particular project.

For example, Acme can subscribe to the notification service at the organization level, then enable it in the Acme Shop project. Creating a project or connecting an email provider does not perform those two steps for you.

## Read the service state

| State shown | Meaning for setup |
| --- | --- |
| Not subscribed | Review the organization service card and available subscription action |
| Service not enabled | The project still needs service enablement |
| Enabled | The service is enabled for this project; review any separate billing state |
| Org Billing paused / Project Billing paused | Review the indicated billing context with the account owner |
| Coming soon | The listed service is not offered through the normal subscribe action |

The current state and available actions in your account are the source of truth for access and billing.

## Similar names with different meanings

A **provider integration** connects a delivery vendor to a channel. A **Service** notification type, shown as Services in Send Policy, classifies a notification. Neither is the same as subscribing to a Dokaai service.
