---

Customer data and recipient selection serve different purposes. A pool holds customer records. A target list or cohort identifies an audience drawn from a pool. A handler can also accept recipients directly in a trigger payload.

| Object or mode | Use it for | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer pool | Customer records and their attributes | [Manage pools](/customers/pools) |
| Target list | Explicitly associated customer membership | [Target lists](/customers/target-lists) |
| Customer cohort | A saved cohort definition with an assigned query and sample inspection | [Customer cohorts](/customers/target-lists#cohorts) |
| Payload-based recipients | Contact and personalization values supplied for a send | [Configure recipients](/build/recipients) |

## Choose a recipient source

Use a target list when you need explicit membership. Use a cohort when you need to define a query and inspect matching sample data. Use payload-based recipients when your calling application supplies the contacts for that trigger.

Handler creation requires a customer-pool selection. Preparing list or cohort membership is optional when you choose payload-based recipients; do not confuse that optional audience preparation with the required pool association.

Preference groups and topics describe communication choices. They do not act as target lists or select the recipient audience. See [Preference groups and topics](/preferences/groups-and-topics).
