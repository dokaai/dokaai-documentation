Dokaai lets a team configure notification content, recipients, delivery channels and communication choices, then trigger notifications and inspect their execution. Start with the objects below; the task guides explain how to configure each one.

## Your account and product

| Concept | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Organization](/concepts/organization) | Your company/team account and organization settings | Acme |
| [Project](/concepts/organization#projects) | The project context containing handlers, audience work and members | Acme Shop notifications |
| [Product space](/concepts/product-spaces) | The app or brand associated with one or more projects | Acme Shop |
| [Service](/concepts/services) | A Dokaai product subscribed to by the organization and enabled in the project | Notification service |

Select an organization, create/select a project, associate its product space and enable its service. [Account setup](/account/setup) gives the sequence. A product-space association and service enablement are separate steps.

## Follow one example

Suppose Acme wants to send order-confirmation emails. It prepares the Acme Shop project and product space, enables the notification service, and connects its email provider. It selects a customer pool, creates an order-confirmation handler, chooses recipients and writes an email template. A trigger supplies the values for a particular order. The resulting event lets the operator inspect that run.

The same handler can be used for later orders. Its reusable configuration, the values supplied for a run, and that run's event record are separate things.

## Common distinctions

- **Handler versus event:** configuration versus one triggered run.
- **Event versus background job:** notification execution versus data work such as CSV imports or cohort queries.
- **Target list versus preference topic:** recipient selection versus a communication choice.
- **Service versus provider:** a Dokaai product subscription versus a connection to a delivery vendor.
- **Saved versus published:** editing content/configuration versus making the relevant item published; neither sends a notification by itself.
