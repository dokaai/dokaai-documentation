---
title: "Customer preferences integration"
description: "Build a customer preference screen from existing group/topic APIs."
---

A customer preference screen lets an authenticated customer review and update communication choices inside your application. It is separate from dashboard configuration of preference groups/topics and the hosted unsubscribe page.

## Prepare configuration and authentication

Create the relevant [groups and topics](/preferences/groups-and-topics), associate them with [handlers](/preferences/groups-and-topics#handler-preferences), and implement [Customer authentication](/developers/customer-authentication). Keep the customer identifier and product-space signing context consistent across those calls.

## Build the screen

1. Fetch the current customer's choices using [Get Customer Preferences](/api-reference/customer-preferences/get-customer-preferences).
2. Render the returned group/topic/channel choices and their saved state. Use the returned identifiers rather than deriving them from display labels.
3. Submit a group-level change through [Save Group Customer Preference](/api-reference/customer-preferences/save-group-customer-preference), or a topic-level change through [Save Topic Customer Preference](/api-reference/customer-preferences/save-topic-customer-preference).
4. Handle the response and reload or reconcile the displayed choices so the UI reflects the saved result.
5. Test with a controlled customer and the relevant handler configuration.

Use the generated operations for exact request/response fields. A checked box in local UI state is not confirmation that the preference was saved. Handle authorization errors, expiry and save failures without discarding the customer's previous displayed state prematurely.

## Hosted unsubscribe and policy

Use [Unsubscribe experience](/preferences/unsubscribe) to understand the separate token-based opt-out path. [Preferences and send policy](/concepts/preferences-and-policy) explains how audience selection, communication choices and organization rules differ.

Validate the accepted customer-authentication and preference contract for your deployment before release; do not infer that a subtype bypass overrides a saved customer opt-out.
